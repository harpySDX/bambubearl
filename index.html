Custom orders

Have a model? We'll print it.

Send us a link to an STL or 3MF file, or describe what you need and we can help model it. Good for class projects, replacement parts, cosplay pieces, club and team gear.

  • Max part size 220 × 220 × 250 mm. Bigger pieces can be split and glued.
  • We check every file before printing and tell you if it needs changes.
  • We post a price in your account. Nothing is printed until you accept it.
